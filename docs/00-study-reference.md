# 00 · 拆参考视频 / Study a reference

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english)

## 中文

做之前先找一条你想做成那种感觉的爆款视频，**逐帧拆**，把它的运镜和表演规律写下来。后面写梗、写镜头都照着这份规律来。只学规律，不搬画面、不搬梗。

### 怎么拆

1. 先找出所有硬切点（画面突然换掉的地方），把视频分成一个个镜头
2. 每个镜头取 **开头、中间、结尾** 三帧（想看细一点就取 8 帧）
3. 每个镜头写 4 件事：

| 机位在哪 | 镜头怎么动 | 人做了什么 | 结果是什么 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 例：藏在柱子后面，柱子挡住画面一侧 | 基本固定，轻微推近 | 摄影师走过去摆正木乃伊的头，再走回摄像机后面 | 木乃伊端正坐好，拍照 |

4. 全部写完后，归纳成「运镜」「表演」两类规律

### 我们从参考视频里拆出来的规律

我们参考的是一条「古埃及人做现代事」的爆款短视频（7 个梗、十几个镜头、约 20 秒，没有台词，只有配乐）。拆完总结如下：

**运镜**
- **前景遮挡**：机位常常藏在柱子、木桩、石块后面，前景物虚焦挡住画面一侧，像偷拍的纪录片
- **低机位跟拍**：镜头和人平行移动，人走出（骑出）画面这个镜头才结束
- **跟着物体走**：东西往上飞，镜头跟着往上摇；下一个镜头接着拍它落下
- **遮挡转场**：镜头最后，前景大物体从镜头前划过把画面挡满，直接切下一个镜头
- **一个梗两个以上机位**：先全景交代，再切近一点拍同一个动作
- **推近慢而稳**：从全景慢慢推到反应或结果上

**表演**
- **一本正经**：所有人都把怪事当成日常，没有人对镜头挤眉弄眼。好笑的是「他们太认真了」
- **动作做完整**：蓄力 → 出手 → 看结果，从开始拍到结束
- **动作 → 结果 → 反应**：投篮没进，下一个镜头人背对镜头默默走开
- **背景的人照常过日子**，或者轻微反应（被水喷到低头躲开）
- **镜头结尾人走出画面**，结束得自然，也好剪

> 写提示词时，第 1 条「前景遮挡」和表演的「一本正经」最容易被忽略，也最影响像不像。

## English

Before writing anything, pick a viral short whose feel you want and **take it apart frame by frame**. Write down its camera and acting rules, then follow them when you write gags and shots. Learn the rules; never copy its footage or its jokes.

### How

1. Find every hard cut and split the video into shots
2. Grab the **first, middle and last** frame of each shot (8 frames if you want more detail)
3. For each shot, write down: where the camera is · how it moves · what the person does · what the result is
4. Group what you found into "camera" and "acting" rules

### What we learned from our reference

Our reference was a viral "ancient Egyptians doing modern things" short (7 gags, a dozen-plus shots, about 20 s, no dialogue, music only).

**Camera**
- **Foreground occlusion**: the camera hides behind a column, post or block that sits out of focus on one side, like a candid documentary
- **Low tracking shots** moving alongside the subject; the shot ends when they ride out of frame
- **Follow the object**: something flies up, the camera tilts up with it; the next shot catches it coming down
- **Wipe transitions**: a big foreground object sweeps past and fills the frame, then hard cut
- **Two or more angles per gag**: wide to set it up, then closer on the same action
- **Slow, steady push-ins** onto the reaction or result

**Acting**
- **Deadpan**: everyone treats the absurd thing as normal; nobody mugs for the camera. The joke is how seriously they take it
- **Full actions**: wind-up → action → look at the result
- **Action → result → reaction**: the shot misses, next shot he walks away with his back to us
- **Background people carry on**, or react a little (duck from the spray)
- **Shots end with people walking out of frame**, which ends them naturally and makes them easy to cut

> Foreground occlusion and deadpan acting are the two things prompts most often leave out, and they matter most.
